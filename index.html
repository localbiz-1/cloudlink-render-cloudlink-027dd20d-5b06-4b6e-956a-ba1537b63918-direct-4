Frequently Asked Questions

What should I bring to my first med spa consultation?

Bringing a list of current medications, relevant medical records, and photos of your areas of concern helps the provider fully understand your health background and aesthetic goals, enabling a more precise treatment plan.

How long does the initial consultation usually take?

The first appointment typically lasts from 30 to 60 minutes, providing ample time for a comprehensive discussion, examination, and personalized recommendations without feeling rushed.

Can I receive treatment during my first visit?

Depending on your medical history and the treatments discussed, some patients may have procedures done during the initial appointment, while others might be scheduled for a follow-up to ensure safety and optimal timing.

Are Botox injections safe for everyone?

While Botox is safe for most adults, it is not recommended for pregnant or breastfeeding women, individuals with certain neuromuscular disorders, or those allergic to any ingredients in the injection formulation.

When will I see results after Botox treatment?

Results generally begin to appear within 3 to 5 days after the injection, with full effects visible after about two weeks, varying slightly between individuals.

What should I avoid before and after Botox injections?

To minimize bruising and maximize effectiveness, it’s advisable to avoid alcohol, blood-thinning medications, and excessive sun exposure in the days surrounding your treatment.

How often should I schedule Botox treatments?

Most patients find that treatments every 3 to 4 months help maintain their desired results, though the exact frequency can be tailored based on your response and goals.
